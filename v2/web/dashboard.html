Read this small.

  • 32 settled legs across 19 bets. That is a sample that cannot separate an edge from a good month. The negative results this project measured elsewhere rest on thousands of matches and held-out seasons; a run this short is not evidence against them, or for them.
  • The draw run is 6 of 8 against a market-implied 28% (the mean of 1/odds across those prices, vig included). At that rate a run this good or better comes up about once in 133 — which sounds decisive and is not, on 8 bets. Backing every draw across thousands of matches loses money; nothing here changes that.
  • 1 legs are pending — recorded when the pick was issued, not yet played. They count as neither wins nor losses and contribute nothing to any P&L figure here. A slip is only scored once every one of its legs has a result.
  • Picks are recorded when issued and settled by a scheduled job, so the record cannot be revised upward later and a losing week cannot quietly go missing.
